Settings

Everything saves as you change it.

Welcome

Two settings are worth a look before you start, especially if someone else will use this browser. You can change both later.

Keep bookmarked pages

Bookmarked pages stay in your history, even on hidden sites. Off to start with.

Sign me out and clear site data

Deletes cookies, local storage and cached files for hidden sites, so you have to log in again. Off to start with.

Your existing history isn't touched until the next browser start or Clean now. New visits to hidden sites are removed as you browse.

Sites to hide

Anything you open on these sites is removed from your history, subdomains included.

One domain per line.

Wait before removing

Keep a hidden site's pages for a while so back/forward and recently visited keep working. Searches and keywords are always removed right away.

Only after I close the site

Keep a site's pages while any of its tabs is open, then remove them once the last one closes or moves on. Combined with the wait above, both must be over. Clean now ignores both and removes everything.

Remove their downloads from the list

Your files stay where they are. Only the entry on Chrome's downloads page goes.

Sign me out and clear site data

Deletes cookies, local storage and cached files for these sites on startup, on scheduled sweeps and on Clean now. You'll have to log in again.

Search engines

Searches made on the engines you pick are removed from your history.

Automation

Live deletion and the startup cleanup always run. These add extra safety nets.

Re-sweep history

A periodic cleanup while Chrome is open.

Clean now shortcut

Runs a full cleanup from the keyboard. The number of removed entries flashes on the toolbar icon.

Not set
Ask before large cleanups

Clean now asks first when it would remove more entries than this. Set 0 to never ask. The keyboard shortcut opens Preview instead.

Deep cleanexperimental

Also runs Chrome's own history clear right after a search is removed. Try it if searches still appear as address bar suggestions. It removes all history from about a second before the search, which is normally just the search page.

Preview

See what Clean now would remove, including pages held back by the wait settings. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and deletion can't be undone.

Activity

What the latest cleanups removed. Only counts and site names are kept, never full addresses or page titles.

Backup

Move your settings to another computer. The removed-entries counter isn't included.
